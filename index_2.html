What Madechiel is

Madechiel is a skin booster developed by Dongkook Pharmaceutical, the Korean company behind the Madecassol centella brand. It pairs water-soluble chitosan with centella asiatica extract and vitamin B12.

Clinics use it alongside microneedling, fractional laser, RF and ultrasound treatments. Supporting ingredients include hyaluronic acid, niacinamide, tranexamic acid and allantoin.

For professional use. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and your local regulations.
